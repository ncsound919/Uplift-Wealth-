# Overlay Wealth — Institutional One-Pager

**For:** HBCUs, Black fraternities/sororities, churches, community organizations, and workforce-development programs.

## Mission
Overlay Wealth is a financial literacy platform built **for Black communities** — turning generations of economic exclusion into ownership. We teach credit, investing, real estate, business, and group economics through courses, simulations, and community cohorts.

## What You Get
- **Curriculum (15 modules + Wealth Building chapters)** — beginner to expert, culturally grounded (Black Wall Street, redlining, credit invisibility, group economics)
- **Classroom mode** — create a cohort, assign a curriculum, track per-member completion on a leaderboard
- **Interactive tools** — trading/underwriting/fraud simulators, quant calculators (Black-Scholes, MPT), business & real estate analyzers
- **Certificates** — verifiable completion credentials for learners
- **Community** — lesson discussions, cohorts (church / HBCU / family / club), verified educators

## Trust & Security
- Real accounts (bcrypt + JWT), refresh-token revocation, login rate limiting
- Durable Postgres storage; progress survives across devices
- Opt-in public profiles; private by default
- Error tracking + audit logging; production fail-fast on missing secrets
- A community historically burned by predatory financial products deserves a platform that treats trust as the product.
