# Overlay Wealth — Video Scripts (flagship wealth chapters)

> The in-app `YouTubeVideoPlayer` is already wired to any lesson with a `videoId`
> (6 module lessons ship with real IDs today). These scripts are for the
> **Wealth Building** chapters — the platform's differentiator — to be recorded
> (5–7 min each) and added by setting `videoId` on the chapter.

## 1. Credit Mastery — "Why Your Credit Score Is a Price Tag"

**Hook (0:00–0:30):** "Your credit score isn't a report card. It's a price tag on your financial life. A 760 vs a 620 score can cost you $200,000 over a lifetime."

**Body (0:30–4:30):**
1. The 5 factors — payment history (35%), utilization (30%), history (15%), new credit (10%), mix (10%).
2. The system was built to exclude: redlining, predatory lending, credit invisibility. That's why understanding it is an act of power, not shame.
3. The 18-month action plan: pull reports (free weekly), get a secured card, $20/mo spend, auto-pay in full; add a credit-builder loan at month 12; upgrade at month 18.
4. Business credit: DUNS, Net-30 vendor accounts, EIN vs SSN.

**Outro (4:30–5:30):** "You don't need a credit score to have money. But in America, you need a credit score to keep it. Let's build it on your terms." → CTA: open the Credit Mastery chapter.

## 2. Group Economics — "The Oldest Wealth System"

**Hook (0:00–0:30):** "Before banks, Black communities had banks. Greenwood had 600 Black-owned businesses, a hospital, and a bank — funded by the community itself."

**Body (0:30–4:30):**
1. Susu / Sou Sou / Tanda — the West African rotating-credit system that survived the Middle Passage.
2. The math: 12 people × $500/mo × 12 months = $60K/yr; pooled and invested at 8% for 10 years = ~$870K. The group is 10× more efficient than one person.
3. Modern playbooks: investment clubs (SEC guide), real-estate syndication, group purchasing, crowdfunding (Republic, WeFunder, iFundWomen).
4. Marcus Garvey's UNIA — the principle was sound, the sabotage wasn't the economics.

**Outro (4:30–5:30):** "Group economics doesn't mean everyone agrees. It means resources are combined, decisions are voted, profits are shared. Start with 3 trusted people. Prove it. Scale." → CTA: open Group Economics + start a Learning Cohort.

## Recording notes
- Format: talking-head + simple graphics; 1080p; captions on.
- Upload to YouTube (or unlisted), then set the chapter's `videoId` in `src/data/wealthChapters.ts` (the chapter shell renders the player).
- Add a transcript below the video for accessibility.
